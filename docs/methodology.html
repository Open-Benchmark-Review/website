Methodology

We follow Epoch AI's benchmark review rubric for verdicts, checks, thresholds and sampling. A benchmark is Flawed when defects affect at least 20% of its score.

Open Benchmark Review publishes independent reviews of AI benchmarks against a public rubric, with the evidence for every finding.
